Gaff's folded unicorn from Blade Runner, as 0.18 mm line art, about 8 × 7.5 mm. It faces the wordmark, with its horn following the chamfer's slope. It pairs with the quote as the second film reference. Only someone who knows the film will spot it.

Idea

Round 3's rain, limited to this corner: the same grid and angle, about 15 streaks. It echoes the banner's rain without spreading across the body. It balances the katakana sign at the other end.

Idea

Five 0.2 mm lines parallel to the chamfer, 0.8 mm apart, shortening inwards. It's a plain graphic that makes the cut corner look intended. It's the most restrained of the three.
