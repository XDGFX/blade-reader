Rings

O · The shoulder of Orion

Idea

The constellation at the end of the quote, just above “OF ORION.” on finger 6. The eight main stars sit at their real positions, sized by brightness, joined by 0.15 mm lines. Betelgeuse, the shoulder, has a ring round it. About 4.5 × 8.6 mm, fitted under the chamfer.

F · A fridge drawing amps

Idea

A small current-over-time trace of a fridge compressor cycling. Each cycle has an inrush spike, then a running plateau, then off. That's the kind of thing a strip actually records, and it's the punchline of the quote. Axes labelled A and t. About 6 × 5 mm.

D · Unlit readout

Idea

A three-digit seven-segment display with every segment drawn, reading 88.8 A, like the per-way displays in the banner. It shows what the board does at a glance. About 7.2 × 3.2 mm.
