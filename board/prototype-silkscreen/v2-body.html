Spec plate

Each gap between chevron columns holds one fact: a 0.8 mm label over 1.0 mm values, centred on the slot line above. Revision, derating, nut torque, licence and the target panel.

C2 · Neon sign

Adds

The banner's two signs. A double-line frame like a neon tube round the wordmark, and the vertical katakana sign ブレードリーダー (“blade reader”) down the way 1 ear, in its own frame. The ground nut gets an earth symbol.

Silk limits

The katakana is 2.3 mm Noto Sans JP ExtraBold, drawn as outlines. Its finest stroke is about 0.25 mm, above JLC's 0.15 mm minimum. The frames are 0.2 mm lines, 0.55 mm apart.

C3 · Rain

Adds

The banner's rain: short 0.15 mm streaks at its angle over the empty mask, kept 0.5 mm off every pad, slot, label and frame. It fills the ears and the ends of the band, so the board reads as one scene instead of labels on black.

Cost and risk

Silk is free at any coverage. About 250 short lines; in board/ they'd come from a seeded generator, not by hand. The risk is busy-ness: up close the facts need to stay readable.
