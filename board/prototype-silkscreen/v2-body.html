Final: the ring face for rev A

  • Board: black mask, white silk, lead-free HASL, Economic PCBA, about $28.10 fixed per order. This replaces ENIG for the clamp and nut pads.
  • Wordmark: the banner's BLADE READER outline in silk, 58 × 4.6 mm, centred behind the nuts. No frame.
  • Way 1 ear: the vertical katakana sign ブレードリーダー, 2.3 mm Noto Sans JP ExtraBold outlines in a double-line frame. PROG beside J1.
  • Sensor band: three chevrons per column pointing finger to nut. Way 1's are filled as the sensor 1 mark. A spec plate sits in the five gaps between the columns: REVISION A 2026-10 · DERATE 20 A PER WAY ABOVE 40 °C · OUTPUT NUT M4 1.0–1.2 Nm · LICENCE CERN-OHL-S-2.0 · SOURCE XDGFX /BLADE-READER.
  • Fingers: the banner quote, two 1.0 mm lines per finger, between the panel edge and the clamp pad.
  • Way 6 ear: an earth symbol and GND above the ground nut. Along the chamfer at 45°, "DESIGNED IN / AUSTRALIA" right-aligned against the Southern Cross as dots, 2.0 mm tall.
  • Not used: the edge hairline, rain, a frame round the wordmark, and gold.
