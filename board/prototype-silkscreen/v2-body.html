AF · Designed in, then the flag

Idea

“DESIGNED IN” followed by the Australian flag, 6 × 3 mm. The blue field is the black mask, inside a silk outline. The Union Jack's crosses and the seven stars are silk, in the Flags Act layout.

Silk limits

The smaller stars are about 0.3 mm across, so they'll print as dots. The Union Jack's diagonals are 0.15 mm, JLC's minimum line width. It reads as the flag, but not in fine detail.

AC · Designed in Australia, with the Southern Cross

Idea

“DESIGNED IN / AUSTRALIA” on two lines, then the Southern Cross at its real star positions, sized by brightness. It's the flag's own stars, without the detail that silk can't print well.
