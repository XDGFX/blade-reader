Ring-face silkscreen

Board colour: black or white only

On Economic PCBA, JLC does ENIG on green only. Black mask is lead-free HASL only, and white mask is leaded HASL only. Gold on either colour means Standard PCBA. So the choice is really between three options:

OptionFixed fees / orderAccent and clamp finishTrade-offs
Black, lead-free HASL, Economic≈ $28.10 (−$0.50 vs green ENIG)Silver solder art; tin under the clamp and nutWhite silk. Revises ENIG in JLC fab rules and options. +2 days. HASL is less flat on the ESP32's pads (JLC assembles it routinely)
White, leaded HASL, Economic≈ $28.10Silver solder art; tin-lead under the clampBlack silk. Leaded finish in a boat cabin. White mask shows grime and can yellow over the hot sensors
Black, ENIG, Standard≈ $73–90 + panel (+$45–61)Gold art; gold under the clampBoard must be panelised to 70 × 70 mm (3 up) with tabs or V-cuts round the comb
  • Recommendation: black, lead-free HASL, Economic. It is the cheapest option and keeps the current assembly tier. The accent turns silver instead of gold. The ring terminals going on the nuts are tin-plated themselves, so tin pads are an ordinary bolted joint. Gold would cost about $50 an order and a panel.
  • Under 30 % exposed finish only matters for ENIG. Every concept here exposes under 2 % of one face.
  • Use the toggles to see each concept in each colour and finish, with or without rings and cables fitted. Dashed outline: the ESP32 antenna on the parts face, past the board end.

A · Ledger

Idea

Silk only, with no mask-opening art. The wordmark runs up the way 1 ear. Install facts sit in a two-line ledger in the band behind the nuts, read while fitting and then hidden under the cables.

Sensor 1

A triangle on the bare finger, between the clamp pad and the panel edge, pointing at the sensor. It stays visible with the panel's screw fitted.

Type

Stroke font only: wordmark 2.4 mm, labels 1.0 mm on two baselines. Avoids ·°:% by spelling “40 C” in full.

B · Instrument scale

Idea

A meter scale along the panel edge: a long tick at each way and a short one at each slot. The wordmark is a finish opening in the way 1 ear, the only accent. Text columns start on the slot lines, so the grid is the comb's own pitch.

Sensor 1

A filled dot above way 1's long tick, on the body. It reads as the scale's zero.

Type

SVG wordmark in a wide grotesque, 2.8 mm. Labels in the stroke font at 1.0 mm. ° and · are drawn as silk circles.

C · Current path

Idea

Chevrons in each column show current running from finger to nut. A finish hairline over the ground bus traces the panel edge. A large letterspaced wordmark fills the band behind the nuts. It shows during install and in photos, and is covered once the cables are on.

Sensor 1

Way 1's chevrons are solid and the rest are outlines, so the mark is the decoration itself.

Type

Wordmark as a finish opening, 2.6 mm, tracked wide. Install facts are set as a rotated block in the way 1 ear.
