Parts-face silkscreen

The parts face (tscircuit's top layer) as seen from that side, without mirroring: way 1 on the right, the fingers pointing down. Every pad, hole and courtyard is from the real build. Black mask, white silk, lead-free HASL, as settled for the ring face.

Decision

Silk on this face is free at JLC and adds no part types, so every option costs the same, $0. The choice is about legibility at assembly and how the face looks out of the box. Once fitted, the face is against the panel and the bulkhead.

Stock: what tscircuit draws now

A · Datasheet

Designators

0.8 mm text, placed by a search round each part's courtyard (above, below, then either side), clear of every pad, hole, slot, courtyard and the board edge. The six sensor cap trios share one label each, e.g. C11–31, because three 0402s at 1 mm pitch leave no room.

Marks

One mark per kind across the board: a Ø0.5 dot by pin 1 of every IC, a bar beside every cathode (SS210s, TVS, LED). The stock silk outlines are dropped.

Free space

The JLC order-number marker (JLCJLCJLCJLC, 1.0 mm) and REV A go in the strip above D1 and F1. Test-pad nets are written as a legend left of the pads, in the pads' order.

B · Echo the ring face

Wordmark

The banner's BLADE READER outline at 17 mm, in the free strip between F1 and the test pads, the only place on this face with the room.

Fingers

Two chevrons on each finger between the panel edge and the clamp pad, pointing at the sensor. Way 1's are filled for Sensor 1, as on the ring face. Hidden against the panel once fitted.

Labels

Designators only on ICs, diodes and the LED, where polarity or pin 1 matters. S1–S6 above each sensor. The JLC marker sits in a double frame like the ring face's sign.

C · Block map

Blocks

Corner brackets, 0.15 mm, round SUPPLY, PROG, MCU, MUX, TEMP and each sensor column. Each label names the block and lists its designators, so a BOM line can still be found.

Flow

A small arrow on each finger root reads LINE, the sensor column reads up to LOAD at the nut: the current's path, stated once.

Upkeep

Brackets are hand-placed. Moving a block means moving its brackets.
