Tools and parts

Before you start

You need

  • A computer with Chrome or Edge
  • A USB-C cable that carries data
  • A No. 2 Phillips screwdriver
  • A torque screwdriver with a 7 mm socket
  • 13 M4 × 10 hex bolts, each with an M4 split spring washer
  • 200 mm of tinned cable, 1 mm² or larger, with an M4 ring terminal crimped on each end
  • Two M5 flat washers, if your feed lug is flat

Steps 1–4

On your desk

1

Plug Blade Reader into your computer.

{{art:desk}}
2

Press Install. Choose USB JTAG/serial debug unit, then your Wi-Fi.

This browser can't reach a USB port. Use Chrome or Edge, or the steps below.Open this page from xdgfx.github.io to install.

If it isn't listed, try another cable.

Use a 2.4 GHz network.

No Chrome or Edge? Flash it from a terminal instead.
  1. Download blade-reader.factory.bin.
  2. With Python 3 installed, open a terminal where you saved it and run: 
    python -m pip install esptool
python -m esptool --chip esp32c3 write_flash 0x0 blade-reader.factory.bin
    On Windows, type py in place of python. It finds Blade Reader's port itself.
  3. On your phone, join the Wi-Fi network named blade-reader and six letters and digits. Pick your own network on the page that opens.
3

Open its page and set a password.

The installer links to the page once Wi-Fi is set. Its address is blade-reader-, six letters and digits, then .local.

Log in as admin with that password from then on.

Skip it, and anyone on your Wi-Fi can use the page and update Blade Reader. For your own keys as well, build it yourself.

4

Leave it plugged in for a minute.

It takes a zero for every circuit. The red light flashes fast for {{s:ZERO_MEAN_MS}} seconds, then goes out.

Its page shows Not Zeroed as None.

Steps 5–14

At the fuse panel

5

Turn the battery isolator off.

Warning The + stud is live until you do. A spanner across it can arc and burn.

{{art:isolator}}
6

Undo the feed lug from the + stud.

{{art:lugOff}}
7

Lift off every circuit's cable. Take out all 12 screws.

{{art:screwsOut}}

Keep the screws and their washers.

8

Lower Blade Reader on, a finger into each pocket.

Notice It bends easily until it's screwed down. Hold it flat.

{{art:lower}}

It sits flat on the panel.

9

Refit the 12 screws with their washers. Tighten them.

{{art:screwsIn}}
10

Refit the feed lug. Leave a gap between it and Blade Reader.

Notice A lug sitting on Blade Reader bends it when you tighten the nut.

{{art:lugOn}}
11

Bolt each circuit's cable to its own terminal.

{{art:rings}}

The terminals are numbered. Use an M4 bolt and spring washer, tightened to 1.0–1.2 N·m.

{{art:ringAngle}}

Hold each cable straight out while you tighten.

Clip each cable to the bulkhead within 100 mm, so its weight doesn't hang on Blade Reader.

The terminals take M4 rings for cable up to 6 mm².

12

Fit the ground cable from the GND terminal to the negative bus.

{{art:ground}}

Use an M4 bolt at the GND terminal. At the other end, use the panel's own negative bus screw.

13

Refit the cover. Turn the battery isolator on.

{{art:cover}}
14

After a day's use, tighten every screw and bolt again.

Warning Turn the battery isolator off first.

The red light

Zeroing a circuit

Blade Reader measures each circuit with nothing flowing, called its zero, and takes that off every reading. It takes a zero whenever a circuit's fuse is out. A circuit that has been live ever since start-up has no zero yet, and the red light flashes slowly until it has one.

Pull the circuit's fuse for {{s:ZERO_DEAD_MS+ZERO_MEAN_MS}} seconds, then put it back.

{{art:pullFuse}}
{{art:zeroTimeline}}

Put the fuse back once the fast flashing stops. The red light goes out {{s:BLOWN_CLEARS_AFTER_MS}} seconds later.

Do the same if a circuit shows current when nothing on it is running.

Recovery

If something's wrong

It won't install or update

Flash it through its PROG pads with an Espressif ESP-PROG on a 2×3, 2.54 mm pogo clamp.

{{art:progPads}}
ESP-PROGPad
ENEN
VDDVDD
GNDGND
IO0IO0
TXDRXD
RXDTXD

Swap TXD and RXD. Set the ESP-PROG's power jumper to 5 V. Then press Install and choose the ESP-PROG's second port.

Warning Take Blade Reader off the panel first, or fit its ground cable and run your computer on battery.
